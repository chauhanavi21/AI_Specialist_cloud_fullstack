A real network issue was found and fixed

The initial unrelated pod could reach PostgreSQL: the Sandbox's broad allow-same-namespace policy overrode isolation because NetworkPolicy allows are additive.

The bookstore's three application labels were excluded from that baseline, preserving its behavior for other workloads. The repeated positive and negative probes passed:
